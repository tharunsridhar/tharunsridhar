Who I am

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at Vellore Institute of Technology (B.Tech ’26) who likes building things that actually run: APIs, pipelines, and full machine learning systems, not just notebooks. I’m comfortable going from model training and evaluation all the way through to deployment and wiring the result into a real backend.
