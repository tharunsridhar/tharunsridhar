01 · About

Who I am

I’m a AI Engineer and Full Stack Developer (B.Tech CSE, VIT ’26) who builds LLM-backed products end to end: React, TypeScript, Node.js/Express or Django and FastAPI backends on PostgreSQL and Redis, plus RAG pipelines, tool-calling agents and MCP integrations with LangGraph and ChromaDB.

My projects are tested, not demos. FixItBro is a booking platform that prevents double-booking at three layers, backed by 100 automated tests. Inventra is an inventory API where row-level locking fixed a race condition found in Locust load tests (138–195 silently lost updates per run down to zero) and Redis caching cut 9 queries to 1. Trailmark, my hackathon project, links every AI answer back to its source.

I work as a collaborative teammate and open-source contributor: 21 PRs merged across 10 repos, including AMD’s gaia. Clear communication, careful code review and good documentation are part of every PR. I take ownership of features from design to deployment, keep debugging until I find the root cause, and build evaluation habits into AI work from the start, measuring quality with test sets instead of guessing.
