Who I am

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at Vellore Institute of Technology (B.Tech ’26) who likes building things that actually run: REST APIs, backend systems, and LLM-powered pipelines, not just notebooks. I’m comfortable going from designing the data model and auth layer through to deployment, and wiring a retrieval or agent pipeline into a real backend.

My work spans production-shaped FastAPI and Django backends with proper authentication, role-based access, and auditable data models, and RAG/agentic AI systems: retrieval pipelines, tool-calling agents, and protocol-level integrations like MCP. I’m also curious about secure system design: how systems fail in production, and how authorization and architecture choices prevent that.

Outside of shipping features, I read post-mortems of real breaches (RCE, IDOR, cloud identity failures) to understand how systems actually fail, it directly shapes how I design authorization, data access, and system boundaries.
